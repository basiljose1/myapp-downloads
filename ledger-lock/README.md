# LedgerLock 💸

A private, offline expense tracker for India. LedgerLock reads the transaction SMS your bank already sends (and Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm notifications), works out what you really spent, earned and saved, and keeps everything encrypted on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics, and no internet permission.

## ✨ Features

- **Automatic tracking**: bank, card and UPI messages become transactions with no typing.
- **True numbers**: transfers between your own accounts and credit card bill payments are not counted as spending, so every rupee counts once.
- **Budgets and insights**: monthly budgets, spending by category and merchant, balances, card dues and a month-end forecast.
- **Subscriptions, lending and calendar**: spot recurring charges, track money you lent or borrowed, and see what is due when.
- **Reports and backups**: export your data and keep encrypted backups.
- **Private by design**: unlocks with your fingerprint, face or screen lock. Messages from people are never read.
- **Light and dark themes.**
- **Free and Pro**: free covers the essentials, with a 14-day Pro trial on a new install. See the [Free vs Pro table](docs/USER_GUIDE.md#free-and-pro).
